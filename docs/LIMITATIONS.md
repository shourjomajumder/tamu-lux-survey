# Limitations

- **Phone lux is approximate and relative.** Lux comes from a phone front camera behind a paper diffuser, using auto-exposure settings and frame brightness. The diffuser is not a true cosine corrector. Auto-exposure lag, white balance and lens differences add error, and the per-phone calibration factor was set against a single reference under a single streetlight. Treat values as good for ranking dark vs. lit places and comparing within the survey. They are not survey-grade illuminance. Values below about 0.1 lux are near the noise floor.
- **Single night, single time window.** All data comes from 7 Nov 2026, 6:43 to 8:58 pm. Lamp outages, construction, events, weather, cloud cover and moonlight all affect what was measured, and none of these were logged as variables. Lighting at 2 am, on other nights or in other seasons may differ.
- **GPS error.** The median horizontal accuracy is 2.8 m, but readings with accuracy up to 25 m were kept. Under trees and near tall buildings, positions can be off by several metres. A reading can then snap to the wrong 5 m stretch or a parallel path (sidewalk vs. road centre line).
- **Uneven coverage.** 3 walkers covered 4 loops, so most stretches (3,430 of 3,622) were measured by one walker on one pass. Many have only a few readings (median 4). About 27.9 km of walkway was collected and about 55.8 km of mapped walkway in the area was not. The 8 planned routes for that gap were not walked.
- **Test runs removed.** The 3 pre-survey test sessions were dropped. Any real light they captured is not in the data.
- **Vehicle and spike filters can remove real light.** Frames within 2 s of a large vehicle in view, and short bright spikes, were removed (26,321 frames, 9.3 %). Brief real light sources, such as walking past a lit doorway, may be lost. Stretches next to busy roads have fewer readings.
- **Lamp triangulation is noisy.** The 721 lamps include false positives (reflections, windows, signs, duplicates) and miss some lamps. 154 lamps were fitted to 0 cd: they add no ground light and are probably not real streetlights, or are shielded. Colour temperature is available for only 183 lamps. "Possibly out" lamps (255) are fixtures the camera saw but no emitter was found near. Some are lit lamps that were outside the snapshot view, or detector errors. No ground-truth lamp inventory was available: OSM maps only 17 street lamps in the area, and none were close enough to the survey to check.
- **Simulated light map is a model.** On held-out stretches, the published map's median error is 1.38 lux (factor 1.58x; 66 % within 2x). The physics-only model is weak (median error 1.99 lux, R² in log space -0.36), so far from measurements the map reflects model assumptions: lamp positions, heights, intensities, building and tree blocking. Trees are mostly placed from photo canopy share, not surveyed.
- **OpenStreetMap is incomplete.** Paths, buildings (heights often defaulted to 10 m) and landmarks come from OSM. Unmapped paths fall back to off-network 5 m cells, and missing buildings mean missing shadows in the simulation. No emergency phones are mapped in OSM, and none were confirmed in photos, even though campus blue-light phones exist.
- **Privacy blurring may miss.** Faces and plates are blurred using detectors plus a blanket blur of every person's head zone and every vehicle's lower half. People who were not detected at all, for example in deep shadow, may not be blurred. Raw photos are therefore not published.
- **Object positions are rough.** Activity and detection positions use range from apparent size or monocular depth, so they can be off by many metres. Activity counts reflect what one camera saw, not pedestrian volume.
